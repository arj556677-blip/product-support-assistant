# TechPro Consumer Electronics - FAQ, Warranty Policies & Support Guidelines

## 1. Warranty Coverage Policy
- Standard Warranty Period: All new TechPro hardware products carry a 12-month (1 year) Limited Manufacturer Warranty from the original date of purchase.
- Premium Extended Warranty: Customers who purchased TechPro Care+ protection receive 24 or 36 months total warranty coverage including accidental damage protection.
- What is Covered: Defects in materials, manufacturing flaws, component failure under normal operating conditions, battery capacity dropping below 80% within warranty period.
- What is NOT Covered: Cosmetic damage (scratches, dents), liquid spill damage (unless covered by Care+), unauthorized modifications or third-party repairs, loss or theft, normal wear and tear.

## 2. Return & Refund Policy
- 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee: Customers may return any product purchased directly from TechPro official store within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
- Condition Requirement: Items must be returned in original packaging with all included cables, accessories, and user documentation intact without physical damage.
- Processing Time: Refunds are issued back to the original payment method within 5-7 business days following inspection at our processing hub.

## 3. How to Submit a Warranty Claim
- Step 1: Locate your product serial number (e.g. SN1001, printed on the bottom of device or product box).
- Step 2: Verify your warranty status online using our official Warranty Checker or contact AI Assistant with your serial number.
- Step 3: Submit proof of purchase (invoice or receipt) and description of the defect through the TechPro Support Portal.
- Step 4: Once approved, a prepaid shipping label will be issued for mail-in repair or replacement at an authorized service center.

## 4. Human Support Escalation Policy
- When to Escalate to Human Support: If a hardware problem persists after performing official troubleshooting steps, or if liquid damage/hardware damage is suspected, or for custom enterprise volume licensing.
- Contact Support Hours: Live chat and telephone support are available Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM EST.
- Official Email: support@techpro-electronics.com | Phone: +1 (800) 555-TECH (8324).
- Authorized Service Centers: Nationwide network of certified technicians available for walk-in repair in major cities.
